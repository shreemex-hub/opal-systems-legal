Privacy Policy — Opal Systems

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Opal Systems is a private business-development tool operated by ShreemEx Hub LLP, Pune, India. This policy explains what data the application handles, why, and what we do and do not do with it.

Who operates this service

ShreemEx Hub LLP Pune, Maharashtra, India Contact: deepak@shreemex.in

What the application does

Opal Systems helps its user identify potential business opportunities, prepare outreach, and schedule meetings. It sources publicly available company information, matches it against the user's own professional capability profile, drafts correspondence for the user's review, and — with the user's explicit approval — schedules meetings in the user's own calendar.

Google user data we access

Opal Systems requests access to Google Calendar for the account of the person using the application. Specifically:

What we read. Existing calendar events and free/busy availability, solely to identify times when the user is already committed so that a proposed meeting does not conflict with them.

What we write. Calendar events the user has explicitly approved — creating a meeting, and cancelling or rescheduling one at the user's instruction.

What we do not access. We do not read Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, or any other Google service. We request no Google scope beyond Calendar.

How Google user data is used

Google Calendar data is used for one purpose only: scheduling and managing the user's own meetings within the application. It is not used to build advertising profiles, it is not analysed for any purpose unrelated to scheduling, and it is not used to train any machine-learning or artificial-intelligence model.

Limited Use disclosure. Opal Systems' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Authentication tokens issued by Google are stored encrypted in our application's secured backend infrastructure and are used only to perform the calendar operations described above. Calendar content itself is not copied into long-term storage; it is read at the moment it is needed and used to answer a scheduling question.

The user may revoke the application's access at any time through their own Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately ends the application's ability to read or write calendar data. On revocation, stored authentication tokens are invalidated.

Data we hold that is not Google data

The application also stores business information gathered from publicly available sources — company names, publicly listed business contact details, sector and activity information, and the application's own notes about whether a company is a relevant prospect. This is ordinary business-to-business contact information of the kind published by companies themselves for the purpose of being contacted.

Sharing

We do not sell data. We do not share Google user data with third parties, except for the infrastructure providers that host the application on our behalf and process data only on our instructions. We do not transfer Google user data to any party for advertising, resale, or model training.

Security

Access to the application requires authentication. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Credentials and authentication tokens are held in a secrets store, never in application source code. Database access is restricted so that a given user's records are reachable only by that user.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you by writing to deepak@shreemex.in. If you are a business whose publicly listed details appear in our records and you would prefer they did not, tell us and we will remove them.

Children

This service is not directed at anyone under 18 and is not intended for their use.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will change and the revised policy will be published here before the change takes effect.
